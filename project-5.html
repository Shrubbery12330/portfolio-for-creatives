There are a ton of students who are in a soul crushing dept and are about to go out into the world and find a job. They have spent what feels like a life time cooped up in a dorm prioritizing hard skills. Not worrying about soft skills. For many of them business casual is an alien concept and if they were to seek out advice for soft skills on the internet they could be met with polarizing Internet figures that put emphasis on the wrong parts of self betterment.

GEAR2 is a sustainable business provides curated, donated formal clothing packages primarily to men seeking to entering new chapters of their lives. Often times new university graduates.

GEAR2 sends out a package that includes two basic outfits that would look good on any one. One casual, and one formal. along with a couple accessories and information on different ways you can style them. All the clothes work together and are interchangeable to support confidence and limit concern of poorly paired cloths. The idea is to provide a base for the recipient to build off of and personalize over time by adding and subtracting elements.
